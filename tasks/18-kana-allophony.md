# 18 — Kana that change sound by what follows (ん → m/ŋ/n) 🟠
Some kana are pronounced differently depending on the next sound. Chiefly ん: [m] before b/p/m (しんぶん→shimbun), [ŋ] before k/g, [n] before t/d/n/s/z/r, nasal [ɴ] at end/before a vowel. Add this "how it's said in context" info to the lesson AND the Library page for every kana where it applies. Go through all kana; ん is the main one.
